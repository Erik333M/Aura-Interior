# Hitting a visual direction

Pick one and commit. Mixing two reads as "generic".

## Premium / editorial (galleries, interiors, fashion, architecture)

- Serif or high-contrast display for headings, clean grotesque for body.
- Warm off-white / bone / sand background (`#FAF8F5`), near-black ink (`#141210`), one muted accent.
- Big imagery, large margins, generous whitespace. Full-bleed photography with type overlaid or offset.
- Very restrained color; let photos carry chroma.
- Fine hairline rules (1px, low-opacity) as dividers. Radius 0-4px. Almost no shadows.
- Uppercase small-caps labels with wide tracking (`0.12em`) for eyebrows/metadata.
- Motion: slow, long fades and gentle parallax (600-900ms). Never bouncy.

## Soft SaaS / product

- Geometric sans throughout (Inter, Geist, Satoshi).
- Cool neutral ramp, single saturated accent, subtle gradient meshes in the hero only.
- Radius 8-16px, layered soft shadows, 1px light borders.
- Cards, badges, and stat tiles carry the layout.
- Motion: 150-250ms springs, small translate + fade on scroll-in.

## Dark technical / developer

- Near-black background (`#0A0A0B`), elevated surfaces `#141416`, borders `#26262A`.
- Mono for data and code, sans for prose. Accent used sparingly as a signal color.
- Glow instead of drop shadow. Grid/dot backgrounds at very low opacity.
- Dense information, tight spacing, high contrast type.

## Brutalist / statement

- One heavy grotesque, enormous sizes, tight tracking.
- Flat blocks of saturated color, hard 1-2px black borders, radius 0, offset hard shadows.
- Asymmetric, overlapping, deliberately broken grid.
- Motion: instant or snappy, no easing softness.

## Regardless of direction

- Two fonts max. Choose real font pairings, not defaults.
- Pull the palette from the imagery, not from a generic tailwind hue.
- The background is never pure white or pure black.
- One "hero moment" per page; everything else is quiet.
